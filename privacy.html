Katha Puzzles: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Katha Puzzles is a puzzle game that tells Indian mythological stories. It is made by Sumit Patra and is designed to be safe for children. This page explains what the app does with information. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal information about you or your child.

No accounts, no tracking

The app has no sign-in, no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking libraries. It does not ask for your name, age, email, location, contacts or any other personal details.

Your photos stay on your device

You can choose to turn one of your own pictures into a puzzle. The app uses Android's photo picker, so it sees only the pictures you select, and it needs no permission to read your photo library. A copy of each picture you pick is kept inside the app on your device so you can play it again. Pictures are never uploaded or sent anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Game progress stays on your device

Puzzles in progress, chapters unlocked, star ratings, best scores and your daily streak are saved only on your device.

Downloading new stories

When the app starts, it checks our story website (sumitpatra6.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) for new stories and downloads their text and paintings. These requests contain nothing about you. Like every website, GitHub's servers can see your device's IP address when it connects, and handle it under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We receive no logs or information about who downloads stories.

Device backup

If Android backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include the app's saved progress and pictures in your own Google account backup, so they come back when you set up a new phone. This is controlled by you and by Google; we cannot see these backups.

Children

Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country). The app contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no chat and no links that let a child share information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If the app ever begins to collect any information, this page will say so before that version is released.
